# Reflection Essay

## 1. Academic Development

One challenge I faced during my academic development was learning computer programming. This challenge involved technical and nontechnical skills. Programming requires attention to syntax, logic, and problem solving. A small error in C++ often changes the result of a program. I improved by reviewing errors, testing my programs, and correcting one problem at a time. I also learned to read assignments before writing code. This experience improved my patience and problem-solving skills. I now approach difficult programming assignments with a clear process. 

## 2. Teamwork

I have worked with classmates on assignments and group projects. I describe myself as a dependable teammate. I listen to other ideas and complete my assigned work. I also try to communicate when I have a question or see a problem. Teamwork taught me how different ideas help a group solve problems. One area I want to improve is speaking up earlier during group discussions. I sometimes spend too much time thinking about my response before sharing my idea. I want to become more confident when explaining my ideas and giving feedback. These skills will help me contribute more during future computer science projects.

## 3. Career in Computer Science

I believe cybersecurity fits my skills and interests. Cybersecurity focuses on protecting computers, networks, systems, and information from security threats. I enjoy solving technical problems and learning how computer systems work. My computer science courses have helped me develop programming, logical thinking, and troubleshooting skills. Cybersecurity interests me more than other areas because the field combines technology, investigation, and problem solving. I want to develop skills in network security, system security, threat analysis, and incident response. My long-term goal is to work in a cybersecurity role where I help protect information and computer systems.

## 4. Professional Organizations and Development

I would like to join a professional organization related to computer science and cybersecurity. An organization such as the Association for Computing Machinery would give me access to computer science resources and professional connections. I also have an interest in cybersecurity organizations where members share knowledge about information security. During the next five years, I want to attend technology conferences, cybersecurity workshops, and networking events. I also want to earn professional certifications related to cybersecurity. My goals include improving my technical knowledge and meeting professionals who work in my field. These activities would support my growth outside the classroom.
